# Support

## Reporting problems and requests

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/misty-swan/yunagi/issues) in the public repository (there are templates for what to write). We aim to reply first within 3 business days.

Please include the following.

- The Yunagi version (`yunagi --version`)
- Your OS and its version, and your CPU (Apple Silicon, Intel, x86_64, arm64)
- The kind of Japanese input method you use, if it applies
- Whether Vim mode is on or off
- The steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what happened
- Diagnostic information (the output of `yunagi doctor`; it does not contain the contents of your notes or other work data)

Please do not paste real work data such as your notes.

## Email

If you do not have a GitHub account, or for purchases and licenses, write to [support@getyunagi.com](mailto:support@getyunagi.com).

## Vulnerabilities

Do not write about a vulnerability in a public issue. How to report one is on the [Security](/en/security/) page.
